Educational Physics

Frequency Calculation:
With a speed of 1800 RPM and 4 poles, the generated electrical frequency is exactly 60.0 Hz.
Mechanical vs Electrical:
Because this is a 4-pole machine, one mechanical revolution creates 2 full electrical cycles.
Magnetic Flux:
As the rotor's magnetic poles sweep past the fixed stator coils spaced 120° apart physically, they induce voltages separated by 120° in time.
